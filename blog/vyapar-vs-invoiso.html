Vyapar vs Invoiso

A direct comparison for businesses that want offline billing, local data, GST-ready invoices, and a free open-source option.

Invoiso TeamMay 20264 min read

"Vyapar vs Invoiso" is a search a lot of small business owners run right before committing to billing software for the year ahead. Vyapar is one of the most recognised names in Indian small-business billing — a mobile-first app with a large user base and a subscription model. Invoiso is a newer, smaller, free and open-source alternative built for a different kind of user: someone who wants a desktop-first, fully offline app without a recurring bill. This is a walkthrough of where the two actually differ in daily use, not just on a feature checklist.

The main difference

Vyapar was built mobile-first, with billing, inventory, and party (customer) management designed around a phone screen, and a paid subscription unlocking most of the useful features beyond a trial period. Invoiso was built desktop-first, for Windows, Linux, and macOS, and is free and open-source end to end — no trial, no paywall, no feature gated behind a subscription tier. Neither approach is universally better; it depends on whether your business bills mainly off a phone at a counter, or off a desktop already sitting there doing the books.

A shop counter, two workflows

Picture a small hardware shop billing twenty customers a day. On Vyapar, that usually means a phone or tablet at the counter, invoices typed on a mobile keyboard, and — once the free tier's invoice limit runs out — a subscription renewal to keep going. On Invoiso, the same shop runs the desktop app on the PC already at the counter, creates the same invoice with a full keyboard and a bigger screen, and never sees a "renew your plan" prompt because there isn't one. The trade-off is mobility: Vyapar goes where the phone goes, Invoiso stays where the desktop is installed — though the underlying data is portable through backup and restore.

Where Invoiso is strong

  • Free and open-source software — no subscription, ever
  • Local customer and invoice data — nothing leaves your machine unless you export it
  • Windows, Linux, and macOS desktop workflows
  • GST-ready PDF invoices and UPI QR support
  • No mandatory account for core billing

GST invoicing, side by side

Both apps cover the GST basics — GSTIN, HSN/SAC codes, CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown, and a downloadable PDF. Where they diverge is what sits behind that invoice screen. Vyapar bundles billing with broader accounting and inventory-heavy features aimed at retail and distribution businesses running on a subscription. Invoiso keeps the core lean: create the invoice, apply the right tax fields, generate a compliant PDF, track paid and unpaid status — without the overhead of a full accounting suite you may not need.

Where another product may fit better

If you need mobile-first billing at the till, large multi-warehouse inventory, staff role management across a big team, cloud sync between branches, direct accountant access, or third-party integrations with payment gateways and e-commerce platforms, a mature commercial product like Vyapar — weighed carefully against its subscription cost — may genuinely serve you better. Invoiso is not trying to replace every large retail or accounting feature in commercial billing suites; it is built for businesses that don't need them.

Switching from Vyapar to Invoiso

If you're moving away from a subscription, the practical steps are straightforward: export your existing customer and product list, re-enter that data into Invoiso, recreate your invoice numbering sequence so continuity isn't broken, and run both systems in parallel for a week of real invoices before cutting over fully. Keep a copy of your old data even after switching — GST records typically need to be retained for several years regardless of which software generated them.

Best way to decide

List your must-have features, create a test invoice in each app, export a PDF, check the backup and restore workflow, and verify whether your team can use the software comfortably for one full week of real billing — not just a demo session. The right choice is the one that fits how your business actually invoices, not the one with the longer feature list.
