Why reviews matter for a free, open-source project

Invoiso doesn't run paid ads or a sales team — it grows almost entirely through word of mouth and organic search. A short, honest review helps a small business owner searching for offline billing software decide whether Invoiso fits their workflow before they spend time downloading and setting it up. It also gives direct feedback to the developer on what's working and what needs fixing.

Because the project is free and maintained by an independent developer rather than a company, reviews double as informal quality control — they surface real usage patterns that a closed product's internal team might never see.

What makes a review useful

The most helpful reviews mention specifics: what you use Invoiso for (retail billing, freelance invoicing, service quotations), which platform you run it on, and what stood out — good or bad. "Great app" tells other readers less than "switched from a paid tool because I needed offline GST invoices for my shop and Invoiso handled it without a subscription."

  • What you use Invoiso for day to day
  • Which OS you run it on — Windows, Linux, or macOS
  • A specific feature that helped, or one you'd like to see improved
  • How it compares to a tool you used before, if relevant

How your feedback is used

Submitted reviews are checked manually before anything is published — this keeps spam and off-topic submissions off the homepage. Genuine reviews may be added to the testimonials section on the Invoiso homepage, where new visitors see them before deciding to download. If you're reporting a bug rather than sharing an opinion, use GitHub Issues instead so it gets tracked properly.

Frequently asked questions

Will my review be public?

Yes. Reviews are checked for spam and then may be featured on the Invoiso homepage with your name and rating, so other small business owners can read real feedback before downloading.

Do I need to have downloaded Invoiso to leave a review?

Reviews are meant for people who have actually used Invoiso for billing, invoicing, or tracking payments. Genuine, specific feedback about your experience is far more useful to other readers than a generic rating.

How does Invoiso decide which reviews to feature?

Submitted reviews go through a quick manual check to filter spam and off-topic submissions. Genuine reviews from real users are added to the testimonials shown on the Invoiso homepage.

Can I report a bug instead of leaving a review?

Yes. Bug reports and feature requests belong on the GitHub Issues page rather than the review form, so they can be tracked and fixed. The review form is for general feedback about your experience using Invoiso.
