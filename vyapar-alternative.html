Free Vyapar Alternative for Offline Billing

Invoiso is a free and open-source billing app for businesses that want GST-ready invoices, local data storage, PDF export, payment tracking, and desktop support across Windows, Linux, and macOS — with no phone number signup and no paid subscription tier.

No subscription for core billing

Invoiso is free to download and use. It is designed for invoice creation, quotations, customer management, product records, and payment tracking without a paid tier.

Linux and macOS support

If you use Linux or macOS for billing, Invoiso gives you a desktop app path alongside Windows builds.

Open source code

The source code is published on GitHub under an open-source license, making it easier to inspect, trust, and customize.

The subscription problem with Vyapar

Vyapar's free plan caps how many invoices you can create and locks features like multi-device sync, data export, and more than one business behind an annual subscription. For a small business issuing dozens of invoices a month, that cap gets hit fast, forcing a recurring payment just to keep billing normally. Invoiso removes that calculation entirely — every core billing feature is free indefinitely, with no invoice cap, no trial period, and nothing that stops working after 30 days.

Who should switch from Vyapar to Invoiso

  • Businesses hitting Vyapar's free-tier invoice limit and don't want a recurring subscription
  • Linux or macOS users who currently can't run Vyapar's Windows/Android-first app at all
  • Privacy-conscious users who prefer invoice data staying on their own device, not a vendor's servers
  • Developers or technical teams who want to inspect or customize the billing software they run
  • Anyone who wants to skip a phone number signup just to create an invoice

Frequently asked questions

Is Invoiso really free, unlike Vyapar's paid plans?

Yes. Invoiso's core billing features — invoices, quotations, customers, products, GST fields, UPI QR codes, PDF templates, and backups — are free with no subscription. Vyapar's free tier limits invoice count and features, requiring a paid plan for full use.

Does Invoiso require a phone number or account signup like Vyapar?

No. Invoiso needs no phone number, email, or account registration. Install it and start billing immediately. Vyapar requires a mobile number and account setup before use.

Can I import my existing Vyapar data into Invoiso?

Invoiso does not currently offer a direct Vyapar import tool. You can manually re-enter customers and products, which for most small businesses is a short one-time task since Invoiso's customer and product lists are simple forms.

Does Invoiso support GST invoices and UPI payments like Vyapar?

Yes. Invoiso includes GST-ready invoice fields and built-in UPI QR codes on generated PDF invoices, matching the core billing needs Indian businesses use Vyapar for, while adding Linux and macOS support that Vyapar does not offer.
